Meet Dr. Taju

A Prominent African Voice in French Education


Dr. Taju earned PhD in French from the University of Lagos, MA in Teaching French as a Foreign Language from the same University, BA (Hons) in French from Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, Diplôme d'Études Françaises from the University of Dakar, Senegal, and Diplôme Supérieur d'Études Commerciales en Langue Française from the Alliance Française, Paris. His relationship with the French language spans over five decades.

Complementing his French, Dr. Taju earned MBA from Lagos State University and Diploma in Personnel Management from the London School of Economics and Political Science (LSE), along with Diploma in Web Development from NIIT, Abuja. He attended Continuing Studies for Managers at Cranfield School of Management, UK, and the Senior Course at The Management College, Henley-upon-Thames, UK.

Dr. Taju is the author of over ten books and several journal articles focusing on French language and literature, African languages, creative writing, and the essentials of management. Published works include Fast Track Learning French, Business and Commercial French, Le Discours Commercial en Langue Française and Understanding Fundamental French Grammar (Aide-Mémoire). He has participated in the DELF-DALF programme and Intégrer les TICE en classe de FLE, both at the Institut Français, Abuja, Nigeria. Dr. Taju currently facilitates French language immersion at the International Institute of Journalism, Abuja, while also running a similar course in the city for accounting candidates who require French in their professional programme.

As a Nigerian, Dr. Taju appreciates the unique challenges that Anglophone learners encounter studying French, and he teaches with dexterity, understanding and cultural insights that improve reception and learning.
