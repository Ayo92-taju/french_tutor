About Dr. Taju

A Prominent African Voice in French Education


Dr. Taju earned his PhD in French from the University of Lagos, and an MA in Teaching French as a Foreign Language from the same University, a BA (Hons) in French from Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, a Diplôme d'Études Françaises from the University of Dakar, Senegal, and a Diplôme Supérieur d'Études Commerciales en Langue Française from the Alliance Française, Paris. His relationship with the French language spans over five decades.

Complementing his French, Dr. Taju earned an MBA from Lagos State University and a Diploma in Personnel Management from the London School of Economics and Political Science (LSE), along with a Diploma in Web Development from NIIT, Abuja. He attended Continuing Studies for Managers at Cranfield School of Management, UK, and the Senior Course at The Management College, Henley-upon-Thames, UK.

Dr. Taju is the author of over ten books and several journal articles focusing on French language and literature, African languages, creative writing, and the essentials of management. Published works include Fast Track Learning French, Business and Commercial French, Le Discours Commercial en Langue Française and Understanding Fundamental French Grammar (Aide-Mémoire). He has participated in the DELF programme and Intégrer les TICE en classe de FLE, both at the Institut Français, Abuja, Nigeria. Dr. Taju currently facilitates French language immersion at the International Institute of Journalism, Abuja, while also running a similar course in the city for accounting candidates who require French in their professional programme.

As a Nigerian, Dr. Taju appreciates the unique challenges that Anglophone learners encounter studying French, and he teaches with dexterity, understanding and cultural insights that improve reception and learning.

What I Offer

Three programmes, one clear path

I

Reading & Writing

A1 – B1

Build literacy in French from phoneme and sound production. Morphology with graphemes and word development progress into basic competence in listening, speaking, reading and writing. Skill in sentence structure is developed along with understanding of phrases. Learners acquire abundant lexical items and vocabulary in the process. The objective of rapid enhancement of proficiency in French will be achieved within the target duration.

What the programme covers

  • Phonetics and phonemes — vowels, consonants, liaison, and the rhythms that make French sound natural
  • Guided reading and comprehension, graded to your level
  • Vocabulary building and grammar taught in context
  • Structured writing, from sentence to paragraph to full composition
  • Listening and speaking practice woven through every session

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any prior French knowledge to start? +
Not at all. Dr. Taju works with complete beginners and takes you from zero to confident communication. Your first session will assess where you are and build a plan from there.
How are sessions conducted? +
All sessions are online via Zoom or Google Meet. You only need a stable internet connection, a device with a camera, and the willingness to speak!
Can I book if I'm in the UK, US, France or Canada? +
Yes. Dr. Taju serves students globally. Sessions are flexible and scheduled around your time zone. Prices for international students are available in your local currency. Contact us for details.
How quickly will I see progress? +
Most students notice a meaningful difference within 4–6 weeks of consistent sessions. Progress depends on your starting level, goals, and how much you practice between sessions. Dr. Taju will give you honest milestone targets from the start.
What if I need to reschedule a session? +
Life happens. Sessions can be rescheduled with at least 24 hours' notice at no charge. Late cancellations (under 24 hours) may forfeit the session. Full details are shared when you book.
Do you help with DELF and DALF exams? +
Yes. Exam preparation is one of Dr. Taju's specialities. He completed the French Teachers Programme on DELF at the Institut Français, Abuja, and knows exactly what assessors look for. He will structure your preparation with past papers, timed practice, and targeted feedback.
