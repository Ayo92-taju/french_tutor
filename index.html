About Dr. Taju

A Prominent African Voice in French Education


Dr. Taju's relationship with the French language spans over five decades. He holds PhD French from the University of Lagos, has written over ten books, and has taught from Nursery classrooms through to professional programmes in Journalism and Accounting.

As a Nigerian, he appreciates the unique challenges that Anglophone learners encounter studying French, and he teaches with dexterity, understanding and cultural insights that improve reception and learning.

What I Offer

Three programmes, one clear path

Every programme is one-on-one and conducted online via Zoom or Google Meet, with the pace and materials set around your starting level. Fees below cover the complete programme.

I

Reading & Writing

A1 – B1

Build literacy in French from phoneme and sound production. Morphology with graphemes and word development progress into basic competence in listening, speaking, reading and writing. Skill in sentence structure is developed along with understanding of phrases. Learners acquire abundant lexical items and vocabulary in the process. The objective of rapid enhancement of proficiency in French will be achieved within the target duration.

What the programme covers

  • Phonetics and phonemes — vowels, consonants, liaison, accents, and the rhythms that make French sound natural
  • Guided reading and comprehension, graded to your level
  • Vocabulary building and grammar taught in context
  • Structured writing, from sentence to paragraph to full composition
  • Listening and speaking practice woven through every session
  • Session notes, exercises and resources after each class

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any prior French knowledge to start? +
Not at all. Dr. Taju works with complete beginners and takes you from zero to confident communication. Your first session will assess where you are and build a plan from there.
How are sessions conducted? +
All sessions are online via Zoom or Google Meet. You only need a stable internet connection, a device with a camera, and the willingness to speak!
Can I book if I'm in the UK, US, France or Canada? +
Yes. Dr. Taju serves students globally. Sessions are flexible and scheduled around your time zone. Prices for international students are available in your local currency. Contact us for details.
How quickly will I see progress? +
Most students notice a meaningful difference within 4–6 weeks of consistent sessions. Progress depends on your starting level, goals, and how much you practice between sessions. Dr. Taju will give you honest milestone targets from the start.
What if I need to reschedule a session? +
Life happens. Sessions can be rescheduled with at least 24 hours' notice at no charge. Late cancellations (under 24 hours) may forfeit the session. Full details are shared when you book.
Do you prepare students for proficiency examinations? +
Yes. Dr. Taju trains candidates to undertake proficiency examinations from A1 through C2. He will structure your preparation with past papers, timed practice, and targeted feedback, and you register for each level as your competences advance through the programme.
