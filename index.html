About Dr. Taju

A Promnent Nigerian Voice in French Education


Dr. Hammid Taju holds a PhD in French from the University of Lagos, alongside an MA in Teaching French as a Foreign Language, a BA (Hons) in French from Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, and a Diplôme d'Études Françaises from the University of Dakar, Senegal. His relationship with the French language spans over five decades.

Beyond academia, Dr. Taju is a published author of multiple French language and business French textbooks — including Business and Commercial French, Fast Track Learning French, and Le Discours Commercial en Langue Française — and a trained DELF facilitator certified by the Institut Français, Nigeria. He currently teaches French on professional programmes at the International Institute of Journalism, Abuja, and the National College of Accountancy (ANAN).

As a Nigerian himself, Dr. Taju understands the unique challenges Nigerians face learning French — and teaches with the patience, structure, and cultural insight that makes the difference.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any prior French knowledge to start? +
Not at all. Dr. Taju works with complete beginners and takes you from zero to confident communication. Your first session will assess where you are and build a plan from there.
How are sessions conducted? +
All sessions are online via Zoom or Google Meet. You only need a stable internet connection, a device with a camera, and the willingness to speak!
Can I book if I'm in the UK, US, France or Canada? +
Yes. Dr. Taju serves Nigerians and Nigerian-origin students globally. Sessions are flexible and scheduled around your time zone. Prices for international students are available in your local currency. Contact us for details.
How quickly will I see progress? +
Most students notice a meaningful difference within 4–6 weeks of consistent sessions. Progress depends on your starting level, goals, and how much you practice between sessions. Dr. Taju will give you honest milestone targets from the start.
What if I need to reschedule a session? +
Life happens. Sessions can be rescheduled with at least 24 hours' notice at no charge. Late cancellations (under 24 hours) may forfeit the session. Full details are shared when you book.
Do you help with DELF and DALF exams? +
Yes. Exam preparation is one of Dr. Taju's specialities. He completed the French Teachers Programme on DELF at the Institut Français, Abuja, and knows exactly what assessors look for. He will structure your preparation with past papers, timed practice, and targeted feedback.
